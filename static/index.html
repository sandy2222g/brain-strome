Race Pace Planner

A hilly 10 km course and a fixed effort budget, the amount a runner could spend holding 11 km/h evenly. Where should the effort go?

Speed by kilometre

Model assumption: effort per km is (1 + 8 x grade uphill, 1 + 3 x grade downhill) x speed squared. The optimal plan has a closed form; both plans spend exactly the same effort.
